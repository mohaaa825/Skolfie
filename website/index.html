How it works

Several detectors. One clear answer.

Writing style

About 130 style traits, including Burrows' Delta.

Language model

A DeBERTa model fine-tuned on human and AI texts, including fresh essays from current models.

Predictability

How predictable each word is to two open language models.

Mixed texts

Long texts are checked in chunks. Partly AI essays get a Mixed label, with the AI passages highlighted. (Being retrained for the newest models.)

Reasons you can read

Every verdict comes with plain-language reasons and warnings.

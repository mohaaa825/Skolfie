Responsible use

A starting point for a conversation.

No AI detector is proof.

  • Use it alongside what you know.
  • Longer is better. Under 150 words: warning. Under 30: no verdict.
  • English learners are flagged more often. An earlier version wrongly flagged about 1 in 4 essays by English learners (TOEFL essays). We haven't re-measured this on the current model yet, so an AI verdict on such an essay always comes with a warning. Take extra care.
  • English only, for now.

Privacy & data Draft

Student texts stay private.

Built with GDPR in mind.

  • No third-party AI. Essays are checked by Skolfie's own models and are never sent to OpenAI, Google, Anthropic or any other AI provider.
  • EU hosting. Skolfie is moving to servers in the EU, so student texts don't leave Europe.
  • A data processing agreement with every school. We sign a DPA (personuppgiftsbiträdesavtal) with each school before it starts.
